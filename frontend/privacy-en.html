Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Protecting your personal data matters to us. This privacy policy explains what data TRIBE ("we", "the app") collects, what we use it for, and what rights you have.

1. Data controller

The data controller responsible for processing your data is:
Leonhard Ibounigg
Kapellenstraße 34, 8020 Graz, Austria
Email: tribeapp.support@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

  • Registration data: email address, password (stored encrypted, never in plain text), date of birth (for age verification, 18+)
  • Profile data: name, profile photo, bio, location (city/region, optional)
  • Identity and preference data: categories you choose regarding gender/identity, sexual orientation, and style/subculture (both "how you present yourself" and "what you're looking for")
  • Usage data: swipes, matches, messages sent, support requests
  • Payment data (only for Premium subscriptions or ad bookings): processed directly by our payment provider Stripe — we do not see or store your card details ourselves

3. Special note on sensitive data

Information about gender identity and sexual orientation qualifies as a special category of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR. We process this data only because you voluntarily and actively enter it into your profile, in order to enable the app's matching functionality (legal basis: Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR — your explicit consent). You may change or delete this information in your profile at any time.

4. What we use your data for

  • Providing your profile and the swipe/match functionality
  • Enabling chats between matched users
  • Processing Premium subscriptions and ad bookings via Stripe
  • Handling support requests
  • Age verification (only users aged 18 and over may use the app)

5. Sharing with third parties

We only share your data with the following service providers, to the extent necessary for the respective purpose:

  • Stripe (payment processing for Premium subscription/advertising) — Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Railway (hosting provider running the app, server location EU-West/Amsterdam) — Railway Privacy Policy

Other users only see the information you have entered yourself in your public profile.

6. Retention period

We store your data for as long as your account exists. After you delete your account, your data is removed within 30 days, unless a legal retention obligation (e.g. for payment data, for tax reasons) requires otherwise.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have in particular the following rights:

  • Right of access to the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw any consent given, with future effect
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority

To exercise your rights, contact us at tribeapp.support@gmail.com or use the support form in the app.

8. Storage in your browser

After logging in, the app stores a login token in your browser (localStorage) so you don't have to log in again on every visit. This token is deleted when you log out. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Minimum age

TRIBE is intended exclusively for people aged 18 and over. We reserve the right to delete accounts if we have reasonable grounds to believe a user is a minor.

10. Notice for users in the United States (in particular California)

If you live in the United States, you may have additional rights under U.S. state laws, for example the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). These include in particular:

  • the right to know what personal data we have collected about you and why
  • the right to delete your personal data
  • the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights

We do not sell personal data to third parties, and only share it as described in section 5 with service providers (Stripe, Railway), who may only use it to provide their services to us. You can exercise your rights using the contact details in section 11.

11. Contact

If you have questions about data protection, you can reach us at tribeapp.support@gmail.com.
