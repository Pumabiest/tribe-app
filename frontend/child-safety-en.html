Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 2, 2026

TRIBE (operated by Leonhard Ibounigg, "we") has zero tolerance for any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation ("CSAE") and for any child sexual abuse material ("CSAM"). This page explains our standards, how to report violations, and how we handle them.

1. Zero tolerance

TRIBE is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and over (see also our Privacy Policy, section 9). Any form of CSAE or CSAM — including but not limited to grooming a minor, sextortion, trafficking of a minor, or any other sexual exploitation of a child — is strictly prohibited on TRIBE. This applies regardless of whether an account is actually used by a minor.

2. How we prevent violations

  • Every person must provide their real date of birth at registration; accounts under 18 are automatically rejected.
  • Every profile and every message can be reported directly in-app by any user via the report menu ("⋯" on a profile or in a chat).
  • Reported accounts can be blocked, and their report is manually reviewed by us.

3. In-app reporting mechanism

You can report a profile or a conversation at any time, without leaving the app, via the "⋯" button (reason e.g. "Underage") or block the person. Reports land in our internal review system and are reviewed by us. Alternatively, you can contact us directly at any time (see section 5).

4. Handling reported content (CSAM)

If we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM on TRIBE, we immediately and permanently remove the relevant content or account. We cooperate with the relevant law enforcement authorities and comply with our legal reporting obligations, including to the Austrian reporting office for child pornography on the internet (Bundeskriminalamt) and, where applicable, international reporting bodies such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

6. Emergency

If a child is in immediate danger, please contact the police directly (emergency number 133 in Austria, 112 across Europe, 911 in the US) rather than contacting us alone.
